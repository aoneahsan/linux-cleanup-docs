# White-label and licensing

linux-cleanup is open source under the MIT License: anyone may read, build and change the code. A
**white-label licence** covers what the MIT License does not. We build, host and update the desktop app
and the command line under your product name, mark and colours, and you may use the linux-cleanup name
while you do. There is no source buyout, because the code is already public and stays public.

## What is delivered

| Part | What you get |
|---|---|
| Desktop app | The AppImage and .deb under your name, published to your release channel, with signed updates |
| Command line | The CLI under your name, published where you choose |

Each licence names the domains it covers and includes a period of updates; renewals extend it. The
current prices and update periods are on the
[white-label page](https://linux-cleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label), which reads them from our licensing
service.

## How it starts

1. Send the request form on the white-label page with the parts you need.
2. We reply with a written quote. Payment is by bank transfer; the licence is issued when it is confirmed.
3. You send your name, mark, colours and domains; we build, deploy and hand over the release channel.

The plain-language agreement is on the
[licence terms page](https://linux-cleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms).

## Verify a licence

Every licence has a public key. Anyone can check it on
[verify a licence](https://linux-cleanup.aoneahsan.com/verify-license), and a hosted build can publish its
licence file at `/.well-known/linuxcleanup-white-label-license.json` on its own domain.
