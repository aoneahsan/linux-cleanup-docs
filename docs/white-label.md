# White-label and licensing

A **white-label licence** puts linux-cleanup in front of your users under your product name, mark and colours.
We build and sign it, publish it to your release channel or website, and keep it updated. The command line is
open source under the MIT License; the desktop app and its backend are ours and stay private unless you buy the
product outright.

Prices are not repeated here. The [white-label pricing page](https://linux-cleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing)
asks our licensing service for them each time it opens, so it is always the current figure.

## Two ways to have it

| | Licence | Buyout |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | Builds under your name, run by us | The desktop app's source and backend, handed over |
| Source code | Not included | Every repository that builds the desktop app |
| Exclusive | No: we may license others too | Yes: we stop selling linux-cleanup |
| Updates | Six months included, then renewals | Six months from us, then yours to maintain |
| Ongoing cost | Renewals if you want updates | A 7% royalty on the product's gross revenue |

## What each part delivers

| Part | Under a licence |
|---|---|
| Desktop app | The AppImage and .deb under your name, signed, published to your release channel, updating itself |
| Command line | The CLI under your name, published where you choose (for example your own npm scope) |

## How it starts

1. Choose the parts on the [pricing page](https://linux-cleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing), or open the
   [buyout page](https://linux-cleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout).
2. Continue to the payment page. It shows our Wise details; send the transfer and its reference there.
3. Once we confirm the transfer, the licence or buyout is issued and you get it by email.
4. Send us your product name, mark, colours and the domain or release channel. We build, sign, publish and tell
   you each step by email.

Need something the product does not do yet? [Describe it](https://linux-cleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work)
and we reply with a written quote.

## Updates and renewals

Each licence includes six months of updates from the day the payment is confirmed: new releases rebuilt under
your name, fixes for distributions and package managers that changed. After that your build keeps working on the
last version it received. Renew for 3, 6 or 12 months on the
[updates page](https://linux-cleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) with your licence key and the email on the
licence.

## Verify a licence

Every licence has a public key. Anyone can check it, or the domain a build runs on, at
[verify a licence](https://linux-cleanup.aoneahsan.com/verify-license). A licensed build publishes its licence
file at `/.well-known/linuxcleanup-white-label-license.json` on its own domain.

## Questions

**Can I build the command line myself instead?** Yes. It is MIT-licensed. A licence is for when you want us to
build, sign, publish and update it under your name.

**Do I get the desktop app's source with a licence?** No. Source changes hands only with a buyout.

The full agreement, in plain words, is on the
[licence terms page](https://linux-cleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms).
