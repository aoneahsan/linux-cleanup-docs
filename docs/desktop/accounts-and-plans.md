# Accounts and plans

Every plan has the full all-safe cleanup, unlimited dry runs and the same protected list. What changes
is the number of real runs and the extras around them.

| | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real runs | 15 a month per computer, 15 more signed in | No monthly limit | No monthly limit, every seat |
| Synced receipts (numbers only) | No | Up to 5 computers | Up to 5 computers per seat |
| Schedules | No | Yes | Yes |
| Exports | No | Yes | Yes |
| Team report | No | No | Yes |

Prices and limits are set on the plan records the app and the website both read; the
[pricing page](https://linux-cleanup.aoneahsan.com/pricing) shows the current figures.

Sign-in is with Google only. Signing in adds real runs, keeps receipts across your computers and holds
your plan. Account deletion, in **Account**, removes your account and synced numbers from our servers;
receipts, logs and reports on your computer stay where they are.
