The AppImage now adds itself to your applications menu, with its icon, the first time it runs, and keeps that
entry pointing at wherever the file lives. The open window and the dock show the linux-cleanup icon instead of a
blank one. The .deb already did this. Nothing changed in what the app cleans. The app updates itself.
