`--json-events` streams local, versioned JSON events for `--scan` and non-interactive `--all-safe --yes`.

<!-- truncate -->

Adding `--dry-run` to all-safe reports proposed paths without deleting them. The stream can contain full local
paths, so keep it on the machine. The [JSON events reference](/reference/json-events) and the [changelog](/changelog)
have the details.
