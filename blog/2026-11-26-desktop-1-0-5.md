The app speaks ten languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, French, Brazilian Portuguese,
Russian, Japanese and Urdu. Pick one in Settings → General → Language.

<!-- truncate -->

Signed in, the choice follows your account to your other computers, and account emails arrive in it. Arabic and
Urdu read right to left, with paths and commands still left to right. Help has More from Zaions and About the
developer. The bundled command line is still 2.1.1, and the app updates itself from 1.0.4.
